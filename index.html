Read this first. This plan assumes the model the documents point to: omeprazole 7.5 % enteric pellets made in India, filled into capsules and packed by SULTON MEDPHARM in Uzbekistan, with ROPE Pharma as the ordering party, registered as a generic. A generic needs a bioequivalence study; omeprazole is BCS class II, so a dissolution-only waiver is not available.

The binding pharmacopoeia is ГФ РУз (State Pharmacopoeia of Uzbekistan), which uses European Pharmacopoeia chapter numbering. The Module 1 list is the part that changes most often; confirm it against the Centre's current checklist. Modules 2 to 5 follow the ICH CTD and are stable.

The process

Nine steps from product decision to a signed ФСП. Each one names the artefact it produces and where the OMC-Z draft went wrong at that step.

  1. Product and market decision

    Fix the brand spelling in Latin, Cyrillic and Uzbek, the strength, the pack sizes and the legal roles (manufacturer, MAH, ordering party).

    Output product brief, trademark certificate, supply agreementsOMC-Z brand spelled four ways; pack size 3×20 contradicting 10 per strip

  2. Collect the source technical package

    The pellet manufacturer supplies its formula, process, specification, method of analysis, validation and CoAs; the API manufacturer supplies a CEP or DMF access.

    Output P.1, P.3, P.5 inputs; S-section accessOMC-Z package never seen; pellet composition undisclosed; 270 × 7.5 % ≠ 20 mg in the comparable Comomes file

  3. Choose the pharmacopoeia basis and template

    Decide which monograph the finished-product specification follows in full, map every method to the ГФ РУз chapter number, and record the decision in P.5.6.

    Output written pharmacopoeia decisionOMC-Z USP method merged with BP-style conditions; 5.1.4 cited as a method; Comomes cites Ph. Eur. 8.0 in 2026

  4. Design the specification

    Add the tests the general chapters require for this dosage form, set limits against ICH Q3B and Q6A, and drop tests that do not apply.

    Output P.5.1 and P.5.6OMC-Z no content uniformity, one identification test, disintegration in water on pellet capsules

  5. Write the master method text in English

    Every solution with its dilution chain, every formula with defined variables, system suitability for every HPLC method. This is the controlled source for every market.

    Output P.5.2OMC-Z missing 6/5 factor, inverted impurity ratio, no system suitability; Comomes 920/900 transcription error

  6. Transfer, verify, manufacture and test

    Transfer the methods to the Uzbek laboratory and verify them, make three registration batches, release them to the full specification, validate the process, start stability.

    Output transfer report, 3 CoAs, PV report, stability protocolOMC-Z no evidence of any of these; unclear whether the lab runs the English method or the Russian text

  7. Derive the ФСП and reconcile it

    Render P.1, P.5, P.6, P.7, P.8 and the labelling into the Russian template, then check every number against the CoAs and the source package line by line.

    Output draft ФСПOMC-Z drafted by cloning a bisoprolol tablet document and machine-translating; reconciliation never done

  8. Assemble Module 1 and submit

    Application, licences, GMP, labelling, artwork, leaflet, samples for the national laboratory, the draft ФСП. The Committee reviews the ФСП while the laboratory re-tests.

    Output submission; expert remarks; marked-up copyOMC-Z reviewer flagged template and terminology, missed every critical item

  9. Correct, resubmit, sign, maintain

    Apply the remarks to the whole document, not just the marked lines. After approval the ФСП is the release standard for five years and feeds the lab SOPs and every variation.

    Output signed ФСП; lifecycle fileOMC-Z 15 remarks still unapplied in the 16 Jul version; Comomes carried errors 2023 → 2026 → 2031

Who supplies what

Five parties touch the dossier. The colour of each chip is reused in the checklist below.

API manufacturer

Omeprazole API maker (India)

Upstream of the pellet maker; SULTON never receives API directly.

  • CEP (Ph. Eur. certificate of suitability) or DMF with letter of access
  • API specification to the Ph. Eur. monograph; methods; validation
  • Three batch CoAs; impurity profile; polymorph statement
  • Reference standard source; retest period data
  • GMP certificate of the API site
Pellet manufacturer

Pellet maker (India)

Makes the 7.5 % enteric pellets; owns most of Module 3.2.P content.

  • Full pellet composition with mg per capsule and excipient grades
  • Pellet manufacturing process, in-process controls, intermediate specification
  • Finished-product method of analysis and validation reports (ICH Q2)
  • Pellet release CoAs; forced-degradation study; impurity characterisation
  • Development report: why enteric coat, dissolution method, discriminatory power
  • Pellet bulk hold-time stability; GMP certificate; BE data if it exists for these pellets
SULTON MEDPHARM

Encapsulation and packing site (Uzbekistan)

Manufacturer of record and applicant; owns the ФСП, the batches and the stability.

  • Production licence, site GMP, company registration
  • Encapsulation process, batch formula, process validation on 3 batches
  • Method transfer and verification at its own laboratory
  • Three finished-product CoAs to the full ФСП specification
  • Stability study (long-term, accelerated, photostability)
  • Capsule-shell and packaging-material specifications and supplier CoAs; TSE statement
  • Draft ФСП, labelling, artwork, leaflet, Module 1 assembly
ROPE Pharma / MAH

Ordering party and trademark owner

Named in the labelling as «по заказу»; role must be fixed in the agreements.

  • Trademark certificate for OMC-Z
  • Supply and contract-manufacturing agreements defining MAH, ordering party, release responsibility
  • Power of attorney if a local representative files
  • Commissioning or funding of the bioequivalence study
Regulator and laboratories

Centre, Committee, national lab, CRO

Receive the dossier; re-test; review; run the BE study.

  • Centre for Safety of Pharmaceutical Products: current Module 1 checklist, fees
  • Pharmacopoeial Committee: review of the ФСП, expert remarks, approval and signature
  • National control laboratory: re-test of submitted samples against the draft ФСП
  • CRO: bioequivalence protocol, ethics approval, bioanalytical validation, report

What to ask for first

Six documents that let us trace every number in the OMC-Z ФСП to its source and run the reconciliation by hand. They are also the inputs the QA engine needs for a pilot.

Pellet maker's specification and method of analysis

Shows which pharmacopoeia the method really follows and where the 900 ml, 45 min and 70 % values come from.

Full pellet composition (P.1)

Resolves the undisclosed enteric polymer and the quantities, and the transparent-body versus titanium dioxide contradiction.

Three finished-product CoAs (P.5.4)

Lets every limit in the specification be checked against real results.

Method transfer or verification report (P.5.3)

Tells us whether the Uzbek lab runs the English method or the Russian text, which differ.

Stability summary (P.8)

Confirms the 2-year shelf life and 25 °C storage, and whether a water test belongs in the specification.

Supply agreement naming the parties

Fixes who the MAH and ordering party are, which decides the title page and the «по заказу» line in the labelling.
